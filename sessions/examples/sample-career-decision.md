# Decision: [Example] Stay in current role vs pursue new opportunity

## Intake

- **Decision statement:** Whether to leave a stable role for a higher-growth but riskier opportunity.
- **Type:** fork
- **Options:** Stay \| Leave for Opportunity B
- **Constraints:** 6-month runway, family depends on benefits, non-compete unclear
- **Success criteria:** Growing skills, financial stability, no regret in 3 years
- **Optimizing for:** Growth and autonomy over short-term comfort
- **Impact tier:** significant

---

## 1. First Principles

### Insights

- Fundamental truth: current role is stable but skill plateau is real.
- Assumption challenged: "Leaving always means disloyalty" — reframed as career capital allocation.

---

## 2. Inversion

### Failure modes → flipped actions

| Failure | Preventive action |
|---------|-------------------|
| Leave without runway math | Model 12-month expenses before deciding |
| Stay only from fear | Name the fear; compare to 80-year regret |

## Synthesis

### Head says

Opportunity B compounds skills faster; runway supports 6 months; risks are manageable with financial buffer.

### Gut says

Fear of disappointing current team; excitement about new challenge.

### Integrated call

**Lean leave** if runway verified and non-compete cleared — gut fear is relational, not strategic.

### Next 7 days

1. Runway spreadsheet
2. Legal read of non-compete
3. One conversation with trusted mentor who will disagree

### Revisit trigger

If runway < 4 months or non-compete blocks role, re-run opportunity cost only.
