# Framework 7 — Pre-Mortem Analysis

## Purpose

Gary Klein's method: imagine it's **6 months later** and the decision/project **failed completely** — work backward with scenario detail, then prevent now.

## Core prompt (reference)

> Run a Pre-Mortem. Imagine it's 6 months from now and this has failed completely. Work backwards: every plausible reason why — specific scenarios, not generic 'poor execution.' For each, one preventive action before you start.

## Question bank

1. It's 6 months out — **failed completely**. What happened? Tell the story.
2. What failed **first**? What was the second domino?
3. Why didn't you see it coming **at the time you're deciding now**?
4. What **assumption** killed you?
5. For each scenario: what **one action** do you take this week to prevent it?

## Pushback lines

- "Not 'poor execution' — scenario level: who, what, when?"
- "Why wouldn't you have seen that? What's blinding you now?"
- "Give me a preventive action you can do in 7 days."

## Capture before leaving

- At least 3 specific failure scenarios
- Why-blindspot for each
- Preventive actions (actionable, time-bound)

## Done when

Scenarios are concrete and at least one preventive action is scheduled.

## Skip rationale examples

- Pure reflective choice with no "project" — still run; frame as "decision failed" not "project failed."
